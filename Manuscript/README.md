# Research Manuscript

### Attention-Augmented EfficientNet Transfer Learning for Four-Class Brain Tumour MRI Classification: Systematic Benchmarking, Attention Ablation, and Overlap-Audited External Evaluation

This manuscript investigates deep learning-based brain tumour classification using MRI images, focusing on EfficientNet-B0 and attention-enhanced architectures.

**Key Contributions:**
- Systematic benchmarking of multiple deep learning architectures for four-class brain tumour classification.
- Attention ablation experiments examining the individual contributions of channel attention and spatial attention, alongside their combined CBAM configuration.
- Evaluation of attention-enhanced EfficientNet-B0 for medical image classification.
- External dataset evaluation incorporating exact-duplicate and perceptual-similarity audits to investigate potential cross-dataset image overlap.
